# 5 October 2026: landing engagement refinement

The folder enters the interactive portal Home view with native smooth scrolling
and keyboard focus on the selected control. Reduced motion uses instant scrolling.
The preview's five Home doors and board/submission buttons now select their sections.

Monthly exclusives have their own blue callout. The room has three illustrative
message cards over a new Nano Banana plate, with one brief staggered entrance.
These are feature illustrations, not fabricated member activity or endorsements.
The purchase heading enters once; the compact card puts price and checkout ahead
of a native disclosure containing the complete inclusion list. Sage ticks and
rose crosses replace decorative gold and grey. Portal guide colours, solid
ordinals and section labels are mirrored in the preview.

Quiet motion plates now use their existing still posters. No background videos
or pause controls remain, and no animation library was added. Hover feedback is
available to keyboard users too. Content stays visible without JavaScript.
