mostly hip-hop, trap and r&b right now, with melodic rap, drill, afro and pop on there too. some briefs want loops, some want drums, some want a full beat. the brief says which.

what's a brief?

an artist's request for sounds: what they're after, with references. you make for it and send it straight in. urgent, hot and warm say how live it is right now. cold means closed. picked is one i've picked out myself, and i rotate them every week.

are the briefs real? who are the artists?

every brief names the artist once you're inside. i keep the names off this page. they're opportunities i source myself. most go straight to the artist or their camp, and some come through a label, an a&r, a manager or a producer who's locked in with them. the brief says which. if it's on the board, there's a real door behind it.

what happens after i send?

you get a receipt by email, and it shows on your submissions page as new. i go through submissions every morning and every night. i hear it myself and move it by hand: in review, then sent to artist, then placed if it lands. if it's a pass, you get my feedback on the quality or what needs changing. every send gets an answer. you watch it move on your own account.

what's my credit and split if one lands?

same as any collab. your send is logged under your name. if it's a beat collab, we split it evenly. i handle the negotiation with my team, and you're looped in the whole way.

if something gets picked up, do i meet the artist?

how many people send to each brief?

fewer than you'd think. i only pass on what fits the brief, so good music doesn't get buried.
